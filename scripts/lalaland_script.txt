LA LA LAND

Written by
Damien Chazelle


INT. MIA'S CAR - GRIDLOCK TRAFFIC - DAY

Radio DJ's voice fills the small Honda Civic.

                    RADIO DJ (V.O.)
          It's another hot, sunny day today here in 
          Southern California.

MIA DOLAN, mid-20s, sits in traffic. She's practicing lines from 
a script, glancing at pages on the passenger seat.

                    MIA
          (rehearsing)
          I could go back to Phoenix. I could work
          at my mom's store...

A car HONKS behind her. Traffic moves. She lurches forward.


EXT. FREEWAY - CONTINUOUS

Cars inch along the congested Los Angeles freeway.


INT. MIA'S CAR - CONTINUOUS

Mia's phone RINGS. She answers on speaker.

                    MIA
          Hello?

                    FRIEND (V.O.)
          Mia! I mean, we could not believe what 
          was happening. He just kept going.

                    MIA
          I know, I know, it was crazy.


EXT. WARNER BROTHERS STUDIO LOT - DAY

Mia walks through the lot, coffee in hand, heading toward a sound stage.


INT. COFFEE SHOP - WARNER BROTHERS - DAY

Mia works behind the counter in a barista uniform. SEBASTIAN 
WILDER, early 30s, handsome but disheveled, enters and orders 
without looking up.

                    SEBASTIAN
          Black coffee. Thanks.

Mia pours the coffee. Their eyes meet briefly. Something passes 
between them.

                    MIA
          That'll be three fifty.

Sebastian pays and leaves without another word.


INT. MIA'S APARTMENT - NIGHT

A cramped studio apartment. Mia's roommates, ALEXIS and TRACY, 
are getting ready for a party.

                    ALEXIS
          Come on, Mia! You never come out with us.

                    MIA
          I have an audition tomorrow.

                    TRACY
          You always have an audition tomorrow.

                    MIA
          Fine. One drink.


EXT. HOLLYWOOD HILLS - PARTY - NIGHT

A house party in the hills. Music THUMPS. People mingle on a 
deck overlooking the city lights.

Mia stands alone, looking out at the view.


INT. LIPTON'S RESTAURANT - NIGHT

Sebastian plays piano in an upscale restaurant. His playing is 
beautiful but the patrons barely notice. 

The OWNER approaches, annoyed.

                    OWNER
          You're supposed to be playing the setlist.
          That's not the setlist.

                    SEBASTIAN
          I was just--

                    OWNER
          Stick to the setlist or you're done.

Sebastian nods, returns to the approved songs.


EXT. GRIFFITH OBSERVATORY - SUNSET

Mia and Sebastian stand on the terrace, looking out at Los Angeles 
as the sun sets.

                    SEBASTIAN
          I'm opening my own jazz club.

                    MIA
          That's amazing!

                    SEBASTIAN
          What about you? The acting?

                    MIA
          I don't know anymore. Maybe I'm not good enough.

                    SEBASTIAN
          You're incredible. Don't give up.

They stand in silence, watching the city light up below them.


INT. JAZZ CLUB - NIGHT

Sebastian's new club. It's small but perfect. He plays piano with 
his quartet. The room is PACKED.

Mia sits at a table, watching him perform. She's glowing with pride.


INT. MIA'S APARTMENT - DAY

Mia's phone RINGS. She answers.

                    CASTING DIRECTOR (V.O.)
          Mia? We'd like to offer you the role.

Mia's eyes widen. She drops the phone.

                    MIA
          Oh my God. Oh my God!


EXT. GRIFFITH OBSERVATORY - SUNSET (FIVE YEARS LATER)

Mia, now polished and successful, returns to the observatory. 
She's alone. She touches the railing where she once stood with 
Sebastian.

She smiles sadly, then walks away.

                                        FADE OUT.


THE END
